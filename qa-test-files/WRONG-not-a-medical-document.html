🍋 Lemon Cake Recipe
This is NOT a medical document.

Ingredients:

• 2 cups all-purpose flour
• 1½ cups sugar
• ½ cup butter (softened)
• 2 eggs
• 1 cup milk
• 2 lemons (zest + juice)
• 2 tsp baking powder
• ½ tsp salt

Method: Cream butter and sugar. Beat in eggs. Add flour and milk alternately. Fold in lemon zest. Bake at 180°C for 35 minutes.
